Your options

Three legal routes to a BTech

Which route fits depends on how far you got, whether you are working, and where you live.

Credit transfer through ABC

Your passed courses are stored in the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) and mapped to the new university's syllabus. You join a later semester and study the rest.

BTech for working professionals

AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education) lets approved institutions run BTech for employed people, with classes in the evening or at weekends. You need at least one year of work experience, and your workplace must be within 75 km of the institute.

Work-integrated learning (WILP)

Some Institutions of Eminence, such as BITS Pilani, run work-integrated BTech programmes for employees, usually with the employer's support. What you learn is linked to your job.

Who this is for

This is for you if

  • You still have backlogs and your university registration period has run out
  • You hold a diploma or work as a technician and want a BTech
  • Your old college closed or lost its approval while you were studying

Rules you should know

What the regulations say

We tell you these before you pay anything, so there are no surprises later.

  • At least half of your total credits must be earned at the university that awards the degree (UGC ABC Regulations, 2021).

  • Only courses you passed can carry over. Failed or absent papers have to be studied again.

  • Your credits must be in your ABC account, linked to your APAAR ID. Credits stay valid for a limited period, so start early.

  • BTech cannot be done through distance or online mode. The Supreme Court (2017) held distance engineering degrees invalid, and UGC rules do not allow engineering in these modes.

  • Not every university can accept credit transfer. It must be registered on ABC and have its own approved credit-transfer rules.

  • We never promise admission or a fixed number of transferred credits. The university decides after checking your records.

FAQ

Questions about BTech Completion

Still unsure? WhatsApp your mark list to +91 98765 43210 and a counsellor will explain your options.

How many semesters can I skip with credit transfer?

It depends on how many courses you passed and how closely they match the new syllabus. Because at least 50% of credits must be earned at the awarding university, a 4-year BTech usually needs at least 2 years of study there.

Is a BTech completed this way valid for jobs and higher studies?

A BTech from an AICTE-approved programme at a UGC-recognised university is valid. That is why we only suggest universities and programmes with both approvals. Also check any specific rules of the employer or exam you are applying for.

Can I complete my BTech online or through distance education?

No. UGC regulations do not allow engineering in distance or online mode, and the Supreme Court held distance engineering degrees invalid in 2017. If someone offers you a distance BTech, do not pay.

I have many backlogs. Can I still use credit transfer?

Yes, for the courses you passed. Papers you failed are not transferred, so you study them, or their equivalent, at the new university.

My old college has closed. Can I still transfer?

Often yes, if the affiliating university can issue your mark lists and transcript. We help you request them.
