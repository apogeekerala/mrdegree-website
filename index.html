Before we suggest a university

Not every university can accept your credits

Credit transfer is allowed for all higher education institutions under the UGC Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) Regulations, 2021. But a university can accept your credits only after it finishes its own paperwork. We check five things first.

How BTech credit transfer works
  • UGC recognitionThe university is recognised by the UGC (University Grants Commission).
  • AICTE approval for BTechThe engineering programme is approved by the AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education) for that year.
  • Registered on ABCThe university is on the Academic Bank of Credits, so your credits can move digitally through DigiLocker.
  • Credit-transfer rules in placeIts academic council has approved how transferred credits are mapped and accepted.
  • UGC-DEB entitlementFor online or distance study, the programme is on the UGC-DEB (Distance Education Bureau) list for your academic year.

FAQ

Questions students ask

Still unsure? WhatsApp your mark list to +91 98765 43210 and a counsellor will explain your options.

Is a degree completed through credit transfer valid?

Yes, when it is awarded by a UGC-recognised university under the UGC Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) Regulations, 2021. For BTech, the programme must also be approved by the AICTE. We only suggest universities and programmes with these approvals.

Can all my credits be transferred?

No. At least half of your total credits must be earned at the university that awards the degree, and only courses you passed can carry over. The university maps each course against its own syllabus.

Does every university accept credit transfer?

No. The university must be registered on ABC and must have approved its own credit-transfer rules. Many have not done this paperwork yet, so we work only with those that have.

Can I do BTech through distance or online mode?

No. Engineering is not allowed in distance or online mode, and the Supreme Court held distance engineering degrees invalid in 2017. For working people, there are AICTE-approved weekend and work-integrated routes.

I have backlogs. Can I still complete my degree?

Yes. Courses you passed can carry over, and the papers you failed are studied at the new university. Send us your mark lists for a free credit mapping.

Do you guarantee admission or a degree?

No. Admission, teaching, exams and the degree certificate are the partner university's responsibility. We give honest counselling and support you through every step.
