Protect yourself

Fake degree red flags: 7 signs to stop before you pay

Every year, students lose money and years to fake degrees. If you see any of these signs, stop and check before paying anyone, including us.

Red flags

If you see these, do not pay

  • Engineering by distance or online

    BTech, BE or any engineering degree through distance or online mode is not valid. The Supreme Court held such degrees invalid in 2017, and UGC rules do not allow them.

  • Backdated certificates

    A certificate that shows a year of passing before you actually studied is fake, whoever issues it. It will fail verification and can cost you your job later.

  • A degree without classes or exams

    Every valid degree needs you to study and write exams under the university's rules. "Just pay and get the certificate" means the certificate is not genuine.

  • University not on the UGC list

    Check the university on the UGC website. The UGC also publishes a list of fake universities every year. If the name appears there, stop.

  • Online or distance course not on the UGC-DEB list

    For online and distance study, the university and the exact programme must be on the UGC-DEB list for your academic year. Approval for one year or one course does not cover others.

  • Guaranteed pass, marks or job

    No honest counsellor or university can promise results. A guarantee is a sales line, and advertising one is not allowed under consumer protection rules.

  • Payment to personal accounts or pressure to pay today

    Fees go to the university's official account, with an official receipt. "Last seat, pay today" or a personal UPI ID are reasons to walk away.

The real thing

What a genuine process looks like

A valid degree takes study and exams at a recognised university. These are the signs you are on the right track.

Verify a university
  • You get the university's name, approvals and fee structure in writing before you pay
  • You can check the university and programme yourself on the UGC, AICTE or UGC-DEB websites
  • You register on the university's own portal and get a university enrolment number
  • You attend classes or sessions, submit assignments and write exams
  • Your marks and degree are uploaded to DigiLocker by the university

How can I check if my degree certificate is genuine?

Ask the university that issued it to verify it, and check whether it appears in your DigiLocker account. Many universities upload degrees to DigiLocker through the National Academic Depository (NAD).

I already paid someone for a degree. What should I do?

Contact the university directly using the details on its official website and ask whether you are enrolled. Keep every receipt and message. You can also call the National Consumer Helpline on 1915.

Is a degree completed in less time always fake?

Not always. Credit transfer, lateral entry and recognition of prior learning can legally shorten the time you study. What matters is that you study and write exams at a recognised university under the UGC rules.
