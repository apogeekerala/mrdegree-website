Embassy attestation

Certificate attestation and apostille for jobs and study abroad

Most countries ask you to prove your certificates are genuine before you can work, study or get a visa there. We handle the attestation chain for your educational, personal and commercial documents.

Who this is for

This is for you if

  • You have a job offer abroad and need attested certificates for the visa
  • You are applying to a foreign university
  • You are moving abroad with family and need personal documents attested
  • Your company needs commercial documents attested for export
Keep these ready

Documents you need

  • Original certificates to be attested
  • Passport copy
  • Visa copy, if the embassy asks for it
  • Authorisation letter
  • Passport-size photographs
How it works

The process, step by step

  1. Document check

    We check your documents, the country, and the purpose: job, study or residence.

  2. Notary or university verification

    Some countries also ask the issuing university to confirm the degree.

  3. State attestation

    HRD (Human Resource Development) department for educational documents, Home department for personal ones.

  4. MEA attestation or apostille

    The Ministry of External Affairs attests, or issues an apostille for Hague Convention countries.

  5. Embassy attestation

    The country's embassy in India attests, where required. Some countries add a final foreign ministry stamp after you arrive.

Rules you should know

What the regulations say

We tell you these before you pay anything, so there are no surprises later.

  • We only process genuine documents issued by recognised institutions. We never handle fake or altered certificates.

  • Rules differ by country and change from time to time. We confirm the current process before we start.

  • Government and embassy fees are charged at their rates, and you get a written breakup.

  • Time taken depends on government offices and embassies, so we give an estimate, not a fixed date.

FAQ

Questions about Embassy Attestation

Still unsure? WhatsApp your mark list to +91 98765 43210 and a counsellor will explain your options.

What is the difference between apostille and embassy attestation?

An apostille is a single MEA certificate accepted by countries in the Hague Apostille Convention. Countries outside it need attestation by their embassy after MEA attestation.

Which documents need attestation for a Gulf job?

Usually your highest educational certificate, and sometimes Plus Two and mark lists. It depends on the country, the employer and the visa type.

Do I need to come in person?

Usually not. Documents can be sent through a trusted courier service, and we tell you how to pack and track them.

How long does attestation take?

It depends on the state, the MEA and the embassy. We give you an estimate when we check your documents and update you at every stage.
