How to create your APAAR ID and ABC account

Your credits live in your ABC account. Here is how to set it up and fix the usual problems.

By the Mr.Degree counselling team · Updated 18 October 2026 · 5 min read

If you want to transfer credits from one university to another, your credits have to be in a digital account first. That account is your Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) account, linked to your APAAR ID.

What these names mean

  • APAAR (Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry) is a lifelong student ID, sometimes called "One Nation, One Student ID".
  • ABC (Academic Bank of Credits) stores the credits you earn at universities, so they can move with you.
  • DigiLocker is the government app where your ID, marks and certificates are kept.

How to create it

  1. Install DigiLocker or open the DigiLocker website, and sign in with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
  2. Find the ABC or APAAR option under the education section.
  3. Enter your details and choose your institution from the list.
  4. Your ID is generated. Note it down and share it with your college or university.

The screens on these portals change from time to time, so the names of the buttons may differ slightly.

Common problems

  • Name mismatch. Your name must match Aadhaar exactly. Fix Aadhaar or your college records first.
  • Institution not listed. Your old college or university may not have registered on ABC. Ask them, or talk to us about getting a transcript instead.
  • Credits not showing. Credits appear only after your institution uploads them. Ask the examination branch to do it.
Why this matters

A university can accept transferred credits only from your ABC account or official transcripts. Setting up APAAR early saves weeks during admission.
