Online degree

Online degrees from UGC-entitled universities

Online degrees from universities entitled by the UGC (University Grants Commission) are treated as equivalent to regular degrees. We check that the university and the exact programme are on the UGC-DEB list for your academic year, then help you join.

Who this is for

This is for you if

  • You work full-time and cannot attend college on weekdays
  • You want to finish a degree you left, with credit transfer
  • You need a PG degree such as an MBA or MCA for promotion
  • You manage a home or family and need to study at your own time
Keep these ready

Documents you need

  • Class 10 and Class 12 certificates
  • UG mark lists and degree, for PG programmes
  • Aadhaar and APAAR (ABC) ID
  • Photograph and signature
  • Work experience letter, if the programme asks for it
How it works

The process, step by step

  1. Tell us your goal

    Which course, why you need it, your budget and how many hours a week you can study.

  2. Shortlist entitled universities

    We list only universities whose programme is on the UGC-DEB list for your year.

  3. Compare and decide

    Compare NAAC grade, fees, exam mode and support side by side.

  4. Apply and enrol

    We help with the application, document upload and your ABC ID.

Rules you should know

What the regulations say

We tell you these before you pay anything, so there are no surprises later.

  • Choose only universities entitled by UGC-DEB for online mode.

  • Entitlement is given per programme and per academic year, so check the exact course on the UGC-DEB website.

  • Engineering, medicine, dental, pharmacy, nursing, architecture and physiotherapy are not allowed online.

  • Pay fees only to the university, and keep the official receipt.

FAQ

Questions about Online Degree

Still unsure? WhatsApp your mark list to +91 98765 43210 and a counsellor will explain your options.

Is an online degree valid for jobs?

UGC regulations treat degrees from entitled universities in online mode as equivalent to regular degrees. Some recruiters set their own conditions, so read the specific job notification.

How do I check if an online degree is approved?

Look for the university and the programme on the UGC-DEB website for the academic year you are joining. We show you the listing before you pay.

Can I study online while working full-time?

Yes. Most online programmes have recorded classes and weekend live sessions, and are designed for working people.

Can I transfer credits into an online degree?

Some universities accept credit transfer into online programmes. Your credit mapping tells you which ones.
